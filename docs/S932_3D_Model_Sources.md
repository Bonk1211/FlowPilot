# S932 3D model sources

The simulation model is an illustrative reconstruction of the described S-932 BFS/DJ2200 configuration. Dimensions, placement, internal clearances, tubing routes and exploded offsets are authored for explanation. Original S-932 manuals, assembly drawings, measured CAD and an installed-machine survey were not available.

The model is generated by [build-dispenser-model.mjs](../scripts/build-dispenser-model.mjs). Its exported metadata identifies the configuration, source links and illustrative geometry status.

The [consolidated reference](Asymtek_S932_Consolidated_Reference.md) is explicitly a secondary compilation of AI-generated conversation summaries. Its component counts and configuration descriptions remain unverified against original controlled documentation. The public OEM sources corroborate DJ-2200 features; they do not establish the complete S-932 installation.

The two repository PDFs are competition briefs, not geometry references. Public S-920 and Spectrum II material describes other variants and is excluded from S-932 geometry claims. The guide demonstrates assembly relationships; its order, animated flow, spray and separation do not specify a maintenance procedure or validated physical response.
