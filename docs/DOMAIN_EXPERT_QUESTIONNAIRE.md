# FlowPilot Domain-Expert Validation Questionnaire

## Context

FlowPilot is a decision-support prototype for investigating undersized epoxy dots. The golden demo compares a partial restriction in the dispensing cartridge/nozzle path, a material-viscosity change, and trapped air. It recommends an inspection but does not control the machine.

The current visual reference is a simplified non-contact precision-jetting setup. The model and terminology must be changed if the real process uses needle dispensing or another delivery mechanism.

Please answer briefly in bullet points and correct anything unsafe or unrealistic.

## Questions

1. What exact dispenser, valve/head, and material-delivery setup does production use? Is it non-contact jetting or needle dispensing?
2. What are the correct names and order of the components between the material reservoir and the deposited dot?
3. Is a partial cartridge/nozzle restriction a realistic cause of persistently undersized dots? What symptoms normally accompany it?
4. What other two or three causes should be considered, and how would their visible or logged symptoms differ?
5. What is the safe inspection sequence? Include the required machine state, isolation, PPE, and warnings.
6. Which inspection or maintenance actions may an operator perform, and which require trained maintenance personnel?
7. If an obstruction is found, is cleaning permitted or should the cartridge/nozzle be replaced? What actions must never be recommended?
8. If no obstruction is found, what should the technician check next?
9. After corrective action, what test and acceptance criteria demonstrate recovery?
10. Which machine-log fields are realistically available and diagnostically useful? Include field names and units where possible.

Finally, flag anything in the proposed workflow that would be unsafe, technically misleading, or unlike real production practice.
