# FlowPilot Domain-Expert Validation Questionnaire

## Context

[Expert feedback](archive/expert-response.md) changes the active scenario to the described S-932 / DJ-2200 non-contact atomized flux process with BFS. The five cause families are restriction, atomization, supply, alignment and material condition. See [the critical review](EXPERT_REVIEW.md).

Please validate the revised model, nozzle-only inspection scope, role boundaries, safety wording, product-specific recovery criteria and original exported log examples. Receiving the response does not approve the revised implementation. The questions below remain the review checklist; the blank review record has not been signed.

## Questions

1. What exact dispenser, valve/head, and material-delivery setup does production use? Is it non-contact jetting or needle dispensing?
2. What are the correct names and order of the components between the material reservoir and the spray-covered bump area?
3. Is a partial fluid/nozzle restriction a realistic cause of declining flux weight or coverage? What symptoms normally accompany it?
4. What other two or three causes should be considered, and how would their visible or logged symptoms differ?
5. What is the safe inspection sequence? Include the required machine state, isolation, PPE, and warnings.
6. Which inspection or maintenance actions may an operator perform, and which require trained maintenance personnel?
7. If an obstruction is found, is cleaning permitted or should the fluid/nozzle be replaced? What actions must never be recommended?
8. If no obstruction is found, what should the technician check next?
9. After corrective action, what test and acceptance criteria demonstrate recovery?
10. Which machine-log fields are realistically available and diagnostically useful? Include field names and units where possible.

Finally, flag anything in the proposed workflow that would be unsafe, technically misleading, or unlike real production practice.
